the local brief — your town, in five minutes

A weekly email about one zip code at a time: what's on this week, what changed at the town hall, which road is closed, and who's open down the street.

What's in it

  • Everything on the calendar near you, submitted by neighbours
  • Council agendas, road work and the dates that actually affect a week
  • A directory of the businesses in your zip — free, and not an advert
  • Every edition kept at its own address, readable years later

Not published yet

The paper is built and running privately while the first corridor's content is assembled. It starts when there is enough worth reading in a town to be worth five minutes of a Thursday — not before.
